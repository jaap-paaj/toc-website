A recent [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/research/amper-vier-procent-nederlandse-bedrijven-volledig-klaar-om-ai-zetten) article highlights a painful truth from the Cisco AI Readiness Index report: only 4% of Dutch businesses are fully prepared for AI. With most companies struggling with AI readiness, the gap between planning and action is large. Unfortunately, getting your organization ready for AI will need more than downloading white papers.

One thing that Cisco's AI Readiness Index shows, is that there is a clear need for practical AI integration in business. The Only Constant offers a fast and simple way to bridge this gap: the AI Design Sprint. This sprint offers way more than just strategy. It helps to bring it to life, fast and efficiently.

## The AI Design Sprint helps to

- **Align with your business goals** -- The AI Design Sprint ensures that AI initiatives directly contribute to your strategic objectives.
- **Simplify AI experimentation** -- Create meaningful and smart AI concepts based on business needs and your organizational, data and IT reality.
- **Deliver working prototypes fast** -- Test AI ideas in a safe, structured setting, minimizing risks.

The great thing about this sprint is that it will have your team actively engage with AI and deliver a working prototype. It will show what you could and should do with AI. In short, you're not just preparing for an AI future -- you're creating it.
